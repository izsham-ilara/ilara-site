01 The problem

Three in four players leave. You find out too late to do anything.

It's rarely the game. Nobody can watch a million players one at a time, so retention budget gets sprayed at everyone and the players worth saving walk out unnoticed.

You spend on the wrong players

Most retention budget lands on people who were never coming back, or who were coming back anyway. Both are money you didn't need to spend.

Every week
a fresh set of calls, made by eye, with real money on them

So your team guesses

There are a thousand moves you could make in a week: an offer, an event, a push, a price change. Nothing tells you which one is worth making on Tuesday.

Most of the gap between what your game earns and what it could earn comes down to decisions nobody had the time to make.
