        decide
        

          
// how it works

          
Ilara decides, you approve.

          
Every player is scored continuously against what they are about to do next. Ilara ranks the moves that would change that outcome, prices each one, and shows you the call with the reasoning behind it. You approve, and it ships, always against a live control group so you can see what that move was worth.

          

            
predict()
Every player, scored on what they do next.
Continuously and per player, not a cohort read once a week.

            
decide()
The moves worth making, ranked and priced.
A short list of specific plays, each carrying the reason it was chosen.

            
act()
Approve it, and it ships against a control.
What comes back is what the move earned, not what it should have earned.

        faq
        

          
// faq

          
The four hard questions, answered.

          

            
01Do we have to replace our stack?
No. Ilara reads the events you already send and writes decisions back through the tools your team already works in. It is a layer on top, not a rip and replace.

            
02How fast until we see something real?
The same week, on your own events. There is no per-game model to rebuild first, so the first ranked players come back the day we get an export.

            
03Do we need an ML team?
No, and that is the point of this. The alternative is roughly six months of building and a data-science hire before you learn whether it works on your game.

            
04Is it safe to run on a title that is already live?
It never writes to your game build, only to the channels you already message players through, and every move runs against a control group you can read afterwards.
