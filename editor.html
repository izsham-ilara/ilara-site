        decide
        

          
// the fix

          
Ilara decides, so your team doesn't have to guess.

          
It scores every player every day, works out who's about to leave or spend, and turns that into a ranked list of moves with a dollar value on each. Your team approves the move instead of designing it.

          

            
predict()
Who's leaving, who's about to pay.
Every player scored daily, before they act, on signals your team can't watch by hand.

            
decide()
The week's moves, ranked by money.
Not a chart. A short list of specific plays, each priced, each with the reason attached.

            
act()
Ship it, measured against a control.
Approve and it ships. What comes back is earned dollars, tested against a holdout, not a projection.

        faq
        

          
// faq

          
The questions a technical buyer asks.

          

            
01How much history do you need?
Whatever you already collect. We stand predictions up on your existing events, cold, without new instrumentation or feature engineering.

            
02How do you prove a number is real?
Every prediction is tested on players it has never seen, and every play runs against a control group. What comes back is earned dollars measured against that control. When a test misses, we say so.

            
03Do we need an ML team?
No. That's the point. Ilara is the decision layer a studio gets without hiring one, standing models up in days rather than the six-month integration and the data-science hire.

            
04Does it replace our tools?
No. It reads the same game data your team already collects and writes decisions back into the places they already act. It's the layer on top, not a rip-and-replace.
